## Overview

A versatile outdoor bag designed for the modern urban explorer. It seamlessly
transitions from a compact shoulder bag to a comfortable seating mat, ensuring
you're always ready for a spontaneous break in nature.

## Exploring the Fold

Iterations on geometry and volume — a throwback to a first-year concept of a
foldable object.

## The Concept: Urban Spontaneity

Designed to bridge the gap between mobility and comfort, Poly.Go.Nic eliminates
the bulk of traditional outdoor gear, turning the transition from walking to
resting into a single, intuitive gesture.
