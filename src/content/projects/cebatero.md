## Overview

Mate is the social glue of my Argentinian heritage, an obsession that defines
hospitality. Cebatero reinterprets this deep-rooted ritual through a
contemporary industrial lens.

The goal was to honor the spirit of the "Ronda" (the circle) while making the
preparation accessible to everyone — from traditionalists to modern users —
without losing the soul of the ceremony.

## Engineering a "Fool-Proof" Ritual

Traditional mate preparation is a high-skill ritual that often leads to clogged
straws, inconsistent quantities, and mess. Cebatero re-engineers this experience
into a fool-proof system.

The traditional gourd is completely reimagined into a visually striking,
intuitive vessel tailored specifically for beginners — one inviting centerpiece
that maximizes the Joy of Use.
