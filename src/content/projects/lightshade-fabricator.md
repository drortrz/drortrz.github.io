## Overview

This project explores the transition from digital generative logic to physical
craftsmanship. The goal was to create a "Fabricator" — a single parametric
system capable of producing infinite unique lighting solutions based on spatial
data.

## Technical Logic & Fabrication

Translating any 3D surface into flat puzzle pieces, the algorithm deconstructs
the fluid form into an intersecting grid, engineered for a precise, glue-free
mechanical fit.

## Nesting Algorithms

The system strategically packs 2D components onto standard plywood sheets to
maximize material yield and minimize waste, ensuring sustainable CNC production.
