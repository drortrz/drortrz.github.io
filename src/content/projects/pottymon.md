## Overview

Pottymon is a comforting companion designed to ease the delicate milestone of
potty training. The design transforms a key physiological principle into an
engaging activity: the act of blowing naturally engages abdominal muscles and
relaxes the pelvic floor.

Featuring two independent interactive elements — a wind instrument mouthpiece
and a spinning pinwheel — the toy encourages toddlers to intuitively explore
exhaling. This deliberate separation of visual and auditory feedback reduces
anxiety, creating a focused, relaxed and playful bathroom experience.

## Form & Ergonomics: The Dual Audience

Designing for toddlers requires addressing a dual audience. Pottymon features a
clean, contemporary aesthetic and a refined color palette that appeals to
parents. Simultaneously, its soft biomorphic curves and lightweight materials
are made for a toddler's small hands.

This careful balance creates an approachable object that ensures an intuitive,
safe, and highly ergonomic user experience.
