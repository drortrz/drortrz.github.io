## Overview

A modular wooden toy designed for both children and adults, combining
craftsmanship with playful interaction. Made from a variety of wood types, the
project integrates a range of woodworking techniques.

## The System

Using embedded magnets, the system allows users to assemble and reconfigure
components into a wide range of bug-like creatures — encouraging creativity,
exploration, and open-ended play.

Developed in collaboration with Gal Porat.
