## Overview

Developed during a 72-hour international hackathon at the EGOE HQ in Bílovice,
Czech Republic, as a collaboration between Bezalel Academy of Arts and Design
and Tomas Bata University in Zlín.

Our team successfully designed a line of fast-manufacturing light fixtures for
EGOE. The design speaks the company's visual language and seamlessly integrates
with their existing manufacturing technologies and familiar materials.

## Design & Execution

The resulting family of light fixtures was directly inspired by the construction
and aesthetics of the manufacturer's award-winning armchair. Constructed from
sheet metal and Batyline mesh to ensure safety and stability, the product is
virtually indestructible.

Taking a leading role in the team's workflow, I produced the high-quality
product renders — impressing the company's resident designers with both our
efficient process and the final outcome.

Made in collaboration with Ido Erez.
