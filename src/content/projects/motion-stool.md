## Overview

A versatile, multi-position seating object. Blending CNC fabrication with
hand-woven craftsmanship, it seamlessly transitions from a stable stool to a
playful rocker.

## Fun Follows Function

The tension-cord structure creates an airy, lightweight aesthetic that
encourages active sitting and introduces a sense of everyday playfulness into
the user's environment.

Designed in collaboration with Nevo Gat.
